# Hal Visual Direction Sidebar

Prepared: 2026-06-25

## Intent

Hal should look like a trusted executive operating partner, not Brian Halligan and not a novelty robot. The visual should feel like someone an experienced founder, board member, or operator would want beside them in high-context work: composed, discreet, capable, slightly warm, and clearly synthetic enough to avoid identity confusion.

## Visual Strategy

Hal should read as:

- executive operating partner
- boardroom-caliber assistant
- calm strategic advisor
- technically sophisticated without looking like a gadget
- human-facing Tavus persona, not a Brian clone

Hal should not read as:

- Brian Halligan
- celebrity likeness
- sci-fi android
- cartoon mascot
- generic SaaS chatbot
- butler parody
- finance stock-photo executive

## Recommended Look

Core direction:

- late-40s to early-50s synthetic male-presenting executive advisor
- composed face, intelligent eyes, subtle warmth
- neat salt-and-pepper hair
- clean modern tailoring, no flashy luxury cues
- soft confidence rather than dominance
- slight academic/operator feel: someone comfortable in founder meetings, board prep, product reviews, and strategic triage
- background can hint at a warm modern study plus high-end operating room: books, soft glass, subtle interface light, but no visible private files or real company data
